# PM-1002: TLS Certificate Lifecycle Failure

Related Incident: INC-1003

## Root Cause
Certificate renewal was manual.
The assigned owner was inactive.
No backup owner existed.
No certificate expiry alert existed.

## Corrective Actions
- Renewed certificate.
- Assigned primary and backup owners.
- Automated renewal planned.
- Expiry alerts planned at 30, 14, and 7 days.
- Monthly certificate audit planned.
